In-Match Growth Mechanic


Character Class Skill

Every player has a different playstyle. We hope everyone will be able to create highlights based on their specialty, whether they're a sharpshooter, master medic, scouting sentinel, stealth hunter, or vehicle expert.

Choose one of 5 passive skills: Healing, Combat, Recon, Detection, and Vehicle. Each specialization is divided into C/B/A/S ranks, starting at C-rank. Complete match missions to upgrade and enhance skills.

●Healing Skill:

✧ C-Rank: Use consumables faster.

✧ B-Rank: Use consumables much faster.

✧ A-Rank: Rescue teammates faster.

✧ S-Rank: Rescue teammates faster, and rescued teammates recover additional Health.

●Combat Skill:

✧C-Rank: Faster reload speed after downing or defeating an enemy.

✧B-Rank: Significantly faster reload speed after downing or defeating an enemy.

✧ A-Rank: Recover Health after downing or defeating an enemy.

✧ S-Rank: Recover more Health after downing or defeating an enemy.

●Recon Skill:

✧ C-Rank: Hit an enemy to mark them for a period of time (visible to teammates).

✧ B-Rank: Hit an enemy to mark them for a longer period of time (visible to teammates).

✧ A-Rank: Enemies that hit you are automatically marked.

✧ S-Rank: Enemies that hit you are automatically marked for a longer period of time.

●Detection Skill:

✧ C-Rank: Go prone for a while to detect nearby supplies.

✧ B-Rank: Go prone for a shorter duration to detect nearby supplies.

✧ A-Rank: Scan in a certain range to detect nearby enemies and vehicles, even while crawling.

✧ S-Rank: Scan in a larger range to detect nearby enemies and vehicles, even while crawling.

●Vehicle Skill:

✧ C-Rank: Vehicles consume less fuel and take less damage.

✧ B-Rank: Vehicles consume less fuel, take less damage, and tires are harder to blow out.

✧ A-Rank: Take less fall damage when exiting a moving vehicle.

✧ S-Rank: Take much less fall damage when exiting a moving vehicle.

●Skill Selection: Select or change specializations outside of matches or on Spawn Island.


Energy Rank Mechanic

Everyone can be the #1! Eliminations aren't the only thing that matters in matches. The new mechanic lets you grow through gameplay such as healing, scouting, and driving, so that every player's value is reflected in the match.

● Energy Ranks are divided into C/B/A/S. All players start at C-rank, and can increase their rank by gaining Energy Points in matches.

● Certain gameplay in matches will earn Energy Points. Points can be used to upgrade class skills or unlock higher-rank Temporal Vaults.

✧ Earn points by dealing damage, rescuing teammates, exploring urban areas, driving vehicles, and more. Explore freely in matches for more ways to earn points.


Temporal Vault Gameplay

We cherish every game version we've shared with our players. This time, we've hidden elements from past versions as special surprises within familiar buildings and interactive objects. We hope these unexpected Temporal Vaults bring back fond memories of the past.

As your Energy Rank increases, you can unlock different tiers of Temporal Vaults in matches. These vaults are hidden on the battleground as classic structures. When a player reaches the required level and interacts with them, the structures will dissolve, revealing the Temporal Vault.

●B-Rank Vault: Appears as a small building with dynamic effects. Interact with it to dissolve and reveal an exclusive 8th Anniversary crate containing special rewards.

●A-Rank Vault: Travel through time to a random classic version theme from 2021 to 2024. The vault will display iconic objects from that version. Interacting with them triggers an exclusive performance and switches the interior scene to the corresponding style, before revealing themed crates.

●S-Rank Vault: Classic Erangel theme. Interact with the sandbox to predict the next Playzone location and change the weather in the area to rain. All exhibits in the vault can be interacted with to trigger special effects and obtain extra rewards. There is also an armory providing high-quality supplies.

●Anniversary Lucky Spin:

✧ Obtain In-Match: Anniversary Lucky Spins will randomly appear in A-rank and S-rank vaults. Interact for a chance to get vouchers for different quality items.

✧ Extract From Match: Meet any of the following conditions to extract vouchers from the match.

➢ Condition 1: Meet the specified individual rank requirement when the match ends.

➢ Condition 2: Use the drone in-match to retrieve items early.

✧ Rewards: Open the supply crate in your inventory for a chance to get Legendary finishes, melee weapons, UC vouchers, and other rewards.

➢ From 3/13–4/12, crates obtained have a chance to drop the Jujutsu Kaisen collaboration Aoi Todo Set and Aoi Todo Cover.
