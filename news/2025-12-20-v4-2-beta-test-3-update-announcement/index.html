V4.2 Beta Test 3 Update Announcement

Published: 2025-12-20

Dear Players,

To give everyone a better gaming experience, Version 4.2's third beta test is scheduled for December 18. A new season of Metro Royale has arrived, bringing with it the new Fabled melee weapon, Glacix Arbiter. It is retained between seasons, and you won't drop it when you get eliminated. Every strike has a possibility to one-shot enemies. High-value sellable items can also be found in Vine Crates that randomly spawn throughout the map. Major WOW Smart Assistant update with expanded AI implementation to assist your creations. Unleash your infinite creativity with the new version.

The beta server's exclusive Discord community (https://discord.gg/BDuH8utDBg) is now available. Head over to get official download links for the beta server and exchange tips with other players! The official team will also be hosting events from time to time, so stay tuned!


Metro Royale Updates

Gameplay Update

● Vine CrateVines Crates are distributed randomly throughout the map. Open them for a chance to obtain valuable sellable items.

● New Fabled Melee Weapon - Glacix Arbiter:

✧ Exclusive Special Trait: Has a chance to one-shot PvE enemies and bandits.

✧ Only available in Chapter 30. Has a chance to drop when eliminating zombie-type monsters or bosses in the map.

✧ Retained between seasons, and does not drop upon elimination when equipped.

● New Fabled Sellable Item: Military Drone. Has a chance to appear in all types of crates on the map.

● Updated with new collectibles.


World of Wonder Updates

● AI Smart Assistant Update:

✧ Added an animation generation feature that lets Creators upload videos to automatically generate skeletal animations for custom skills, visual programming, and the Virtual Projection Device.

✧ Added a new 3D grouped object generation feature that allows Creators to generate scenery objects that can be used in WOW through text descriptions.

✧ Smart Assistant now supports floating findow and interface scaling, and each map now has its own edit history (saved for 7 days).


Classic Mode Updates

● Climbing Improvements:

✧ Improved the feel of climbing from window edges.

● Firearm Adjustments:

✧ Increased the base damage of the AKM, M762, ACE32, Honey Badger, and Mk47 by 2.

✧ Increased the base damage of the Groza by 1.

✧ Decreased the base damage of the S686, S1897, NS2000, S12K, DBS, and M1014 by 1, and slightly increased the randomness of bullet spread.


Home Updates

● New Connection

✧ New Fated Connection.

✧ After forming a Fated Connection, you can hold a Fated Ceremony in your Home.
