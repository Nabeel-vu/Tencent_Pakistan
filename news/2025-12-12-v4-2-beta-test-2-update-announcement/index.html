V4.2 Beta Test 2 Update Announcement

Published: 2025-12-12

Dear Players,

To give everyone a better gaming experience, Version 4.2's second beta test is scheduled for December 11. Traverse the Primewood Heart to obtain the Prime Eye. This artifact contains a mysterious and powerful force—it not only grants a bird's-eye view of the battleground, but also allows multiple skills to be unleashed at will, and can even summon corrupting flowers that disrupts enemies. The new themed firearm, Overgrowth - Honey Badger can also switch between combat modes for enhanced firepower, making it an excellent partner in battle.

The beta server's exclusive Discord community (https://discord.gg/BDuH8utDBg) is now available. Head over to get official download links for the beta version and exchange tips with other players! The official team will also be hosting events from time to time, so stay tuned!


New Themed Mode: Primewood Genesis

Map Areas

● Primewood Heart:

✧ Enter the Primewood Heart through the Ishtar Gate.

✧ In the Primewood Heart, you can obtain the rare Prime Eye item. With the Prime Eye, you can activate a bird's-eye view of Erangel and unleash various skills (Barrier, Teleport, Heal, Corrupting Flower, etc.) to control the entire battleground.


Themed Gameplay

● Prime Eye:

✧ Barrier: Summons a huge, opaque shield that blocks shots and cuts off lines of fire.

✧ Teleport: Place two portals that allow one-way teleportation from the first to the second portal.

✧ Heal: Summon a healing zone that continually restores players' Health and rescues downed players.

✧ Corrupting Flowers: Deploys a cluster of plants that can deal damage to players within range. Destroying the plants grants high quality supplies.


Themed Firearm

● Overgrowth - Honey Badger:

✧ Recover some Health and Energy when eliminating or knocking down enemies.

✧ Firing stability increases after standing still for a few seconds.

✧ Switch forms to change the firing mode and consume more ammo to fire explosive shots.


Classic Mode Updates

● Cooperative Climbing:

✧ Reach higher places that you can't get to by yourself where the Sacred Deer passes through with the help of a teammate.

✧ Every player has the Co-op Wristband item in their backpack by default. When equipped, it can be used to trigger cooperative climbing by entering a preparatory state to help a teammate climb.

✧ Lifting Teammate: While crouching, you can assume a preparatory pose and clasp your hands together to lift a teammate. Other players can boost off your hands to reach higher places.

✧ Pulling Teammate: You can assume a preparatory pose from up above to grab and pull teammates upwards.

● Climbing Improvements:

✧ Climbing is smoother now, and the camera movement during climbing has been improved.


World of Wonder Updates

● Template Updates:

✧ The template information layout has been updated for clearer categorization by gameplay type.

✧ Updated some template designs, adding more information including map size, play time, multi-map previews, etc.

✧ Improved the template download process.

✧ Improved template types and the display priority.
