V4.3 Beta Test 3 Update Announcement

Published: 2026-02-12

Dear Players,

To give everyone a better gaming experience, Version 4.3's third beta test is scheduled for February 12. The new themed Temporal Vault is here. In-game treasure hunting awaits. Unlock four ranks of Temporal Vault based on your skill rank. Higher-rank vaults contain all kinds of goodies, including special joystick crates with limited-time items at random. If you pick up a limited-time item and meet the rank requirements in the current match, you'll be able to obtain it outside the match. Small safes have also been added to all Metro Royale maps, with more high-value supplies to loot. WOW now supports real-time voice communication with the AI assistant, for you to better unleash your creativity.


Themed Mode - Passive Skills

● Specialize in one of five types of passive skills: Healing, Recon, Combat, Detection, and Vehicles.

● Skills can be selected outside of matches and on Spawn Island before a match begins.

● Skill are divided into C/B/A/S-rank. You start at C-rank, and can upgrade and enhance effects by meeting certain conditions. When a skill reaches S-rank, you automatically gain a Portable Shield skill.

● The player with the highest global Energy Points will be displayed on billboards throughout the map.


Themed Mode - Temporal Vault

Temporal Vault

As your class rank increases, you can unlock corresponding quality Temporal Vaults in matches. Each Temporal Vault is hidden on the battleground in the form of a classic building. Interact with it after reaching the corresponding rank, and the building will dissolve, revealing the Temporal Vault.

● B-Rank Vault: Appears as a small building with dynamic effects. Interact with it to dissolve and reveal an 8th Anniversary limited vault containing exclusive rewards.

● A-Rank Vault: Randomly refreshes a commemorative vault from any version between 2021 and 2025. It shows iconic updates and core interactive objects from that version. Touch the interactive object to trigger an exclusive animation, change the interior to the style of the corresponding theme, and present the current themed crate.

● S-Rank Vault: The vault contains the 2026 map pack. Open it to trigger a sandbox that predicts the next Blue Zone's location. Interact with it again to turn the area into a classic rainy day. The exhibits in the vault can also be interacted with to trigger different effects.

● Bring Items Out of the Match:

✧ Anniversary Lucky Spin Surprise Launch: The Anniversary Lucky Spin now randomly appears in S-rank vaults and urban areas. Spin the wheel for a chance to get item vouchers of random quality. Pick them up in-game and meet the corresponding rank requirement to unlock an exclusive item crate outside of the match.

✧ Drone Interaction Early Completion Challenge: In addition to meeting the rank requirement, you can also interact with specific drone items in-match to complete the challenge early and obtain the item crate outside of the match.


Classic Mode Updates

● Erangel's Spawn Island has been fully upgraded, immersing players in a military atmosphere.

This update has fully overhauled Spawn Island, improving the pre-match preparation experience. The main updates are as follows:

✧ Unified Style: Spawn Island now adopts a militarized visual design, enhancing the immersive battleground atmosphere and matching the theme.

✧ New Gear Display Stand: Try out recently launched new gear to get a head start on understanding its performance and handling.

✧ Training Facility Expansion: Added a new Shooting Range and increased the maximum number of Sports Car containers.

● Erangel Weather System Update: Upgraded the weather system on Erangel.


Metro Royale Updates

● Small Safe: Will be deployed across all maps.Small Safe: Locations will be marked on the mini-map in real-time. Open them to get high-value supplies.


World of Wonder Updates

● AI Assistant Update:

✧ Added Voice Input: You can now communicate with the AI Assistant via voice. The current version supports English, Arabic, Turkish, Russian, and Chinese.

✧ Added Smart Editing: Creators can give commands in natural language to directly add, modify, or delete objects in game scenes, with support for batch operations. This feature automatically identifies placed marks and smart editing boxes within the scene, helping to achieve more precise scene editing and stage design.

✧ New Smart Editing Box Object: After placing the smart editing box in the scene, you can use the AI Assistant's smart editing function to quickly and accurately adjust the area selected using natural language.

✧ Clear Chat History: Added a manual clear chat history feature, allowing users to clear all chat records with the Smart Assistant at any time.

✧ Real-Time Visualization of Generation Progress: A new resource generation progress visualization panel has been added, which can display the specific progress and status of AI generation tasks in real time, providing clear control over the creation process.
